# Power BI Dashboard Notes

## Page 1: Sales Overview
**Purpose:** Give leadership a quick view of overall commercial performance.

**Visuals**
- Card visuals: Total revenue, total orders, average order value, estimated gross profit
- Line chart: Monthly revenue and estimated profit trend
- Matrix: Revenue by month and region
- Bar chart: Revenue by product category
- Slicers: Region, product category, payment method, and date range

## Page 2: Product and Regional Analysis
**Purpose:** Identify products, categories, and regions that need attention.

**Visuals**
- Top 10 products by revenue
- Revenue by category
- Revenue by region
- Month-over-month revenue growth
- Quantity sold versus revenue by product

## KPI definitions
| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| Total revenue | Sum of sales amount |
| Total orders | Distinct count of order ID |
| Average order value | Total revenue divided by distinct orders |
| Estimated gross profit | Sales amount multiplied by assumed 28% margin |
| Estimated profit margin | Estimated gross profit divided by revenue |
| Month-over-month growth | Current month revenue versus prior month revenue |

## Interpretation caution
The 28% profit rate is an illustrative assumption because product costs are not included. Label profit visuals as estimates.
